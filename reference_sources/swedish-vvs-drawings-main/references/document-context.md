# Document context: precedence, as-builts and DoU

Why this matters to an extractor: a drawing is one document in a set, and it is
not the authoritative one. Knowing the hierarchy tells you when a
drawing-derived number should lose to something else.

### What the technical execution rests on

Ritningar and beskrivningar are the basis for the technical execution of most larger construction projects. Ritningar are built on standards and standardised drawing symbols. Tekniska beskrivningar most often link to AMA.

### What a complete set of bygghandlingar usually contains

- Document list
- Drawings
- Specifications
- CAD or Revit drawings (3D models)

### Drawings and specifications complement each other

In contracts it normally applies that ritningar and beskrivningar complement each other. The prescribed execution does not need to be described in both the ritning and the beskrivning. It is therefore important that the fitter (montören) on site always has access to the beskrivning.

### Precedence between drawing and specification

In the event of a conflict between ritning and beskrivning, the beskrivning takes precedence over the ritning (in an utförandeentreprenad, i.e. a design-bid-build contract).

*Implication:* When automated reading of a ritning conflicts with the beskrivning, the beskrivning wins. Flag the conflict rather than silently trusting the drawing.

### Precedence involving AMA

In addition to the text in the beskrivning, the text in AMA applies. Because a code and its associated heading from AMA appear in the beskrivning, both the text under that heading in AMA and the text in superordinate headings are invoked, according to the so-called pyramidregeln (pyramid rule). If there is a conflict between AMA's text and the text of the beskrivning, the beskrivning applies.

### Other documents that apply on site

There are several other documents to take into account on the construction site, for example environmental and work-environment rules and the manufacturers' installation instructions (monteringsanvisningar).

### Recording changes during construction

An important task on site is to continuously enter changes made during construction on a separate set of drawings, as the basis for relationsritningar.

### Symbol standards and praxis

Ritningar and beskrivningar can be produced according to Bygghandlingar 90. A standard for drawing symbols on VVS-ritningar is SIS 32260, which differs somewhat from the European standard. The industry also has praxis that can differ considerably from case to case, depending on the consultants' and clients' own routines.


## Standards referenced

- **SIS 32260** — Swedish standard for drawing symbols on VVS-ritningar Deviates somewhat from the European standard.
- **Bygghandlingar 90** — Swedish framework for how ritningar and beskrivningar are produced and structured.
- **AMA** — Reference work that tekniska beskrivningar link to via codes and headings. A code plus its heading in the beskrivning invokes both the text under that heading in AMA and the text of superordinate headings, per the so-called pyramidregeln (pyramid rule).
- **CoClass** — Swedish classification system used together with BIP (Building Information Properties) for systematic designations and properties.
- **BIP / BIP QTO** — Building Information Properties. BIP QTO allows quantities to be extracted, sorted and summed for other systems.

## As-built documents (relationshandlingar)

Source: AMA VVS & Kyla 19

- They must correspond to the actual execution and function.
- They must be signed by the contractor.
- They must consist of copies of the documents that formed the basis for executing the contract, with any deviations made during the work entered on them, including a sketch where needed.
- They must contain position data for equipment, units, apparatus and similar whose final placement was not shown in the documents.

- Dimensions and other measurement data must be drawn in and stated so clearly and completely that they can serve as a basis for relationshandlingar.
- A special set of drawings (särskild ritningsomgång) must be kept on site, into which the basis for relationsritningar is entered progressively. The drawing basis must be made in a clear and unambiguous way and with the same symbols that occur elsewhere on the drawings. Text that is a clarifying comment should be circled so that it is not misunderstood.

For changes and rebuilds the contractor most often delivers documentation only of the new work installed. As-built drawings for a building therefore usually consist partly of drawings from the time of the original construction and partly of drawings from later changes and rebuilds. It is of great value to a property owner to have coordinated relationshandlingar for the VVS installations.

## Operation and maintenance (DoU)

The industry standard rests on the fact that both building legislation and work environment legislation require buildings and their installations to have instructions. Responsibility for these existing lies with the property owner (fastighetsägaren). The instructions can take the form of a database or, traditionally, a binder.

The industry standard is *Drift- och underhållsinstruktioner & annan dokumentation i entreprenader*
(Svensk Byggtjänst,
2023).
